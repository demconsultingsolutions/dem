Work we've delivered

A few representative projects. Client names are withheld unless permission has been given.

Policy & governanceIndigenous healing organization

Governance & policy framework for a crisis response service

Challenge
Establishing the governance and policy foundation for a new Indigenous-led, non-police, 24/7 mobile crisis service for youth, individuals and families.
Role
Engagement lead, awarded through a competitive RFP: discovery, policy inventory and gap analysis, framework design, key informant engagement and board preparation.
Deliverables
A policy governance framework with audit, analysis and lifecycle tools applying an anti-oppression and decolonizing lens, and a complete policy suite covering operations, client services, human resources, privacy and compliance, and risk.
Outcome
Full policy suite approved by the Board of Directors.
Emergency managementRegional organization

Regional evacuation after-action review

Challenge
Understanding what worked and what did not when six remote First Nations evacuated during the 2024 season, so future evacuations are safer and better coordinated.
Role
Commissioned to lead the after-action review, including engagement, analysis and reporting.
Deliverables
An after-action report bringing together community and agency perspectives across six communities, with lessons learned and prioritized recommendations for future evacuations.
Capital projectsFirst Nation client

Road & bridge crossing feasibility

Challenge
Restoring reliable access across a creek crossing where the existing route faced structural, land and permitting constraints.
Role
Feasibility lead, responsible for options development, procurement of technical studies and the funding submission.
Deliverables
An options analysis comparing repair of the existing crossing, a new bypass road and crossing, and full replacement, with the technical study requirements for each.
Outcome
Project Approval & Construction

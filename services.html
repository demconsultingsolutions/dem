Pre-capital planning

Capital project readiness

Funding programs reward projects that arrive ready. We take a project from first idea to a funded, procurement-ready scope, and we can stay on as owner's representative through construction.

See pre-capital work
  1. Stage 1

    Define the need

    Needs assessment, community priorities, and a functional program that says what the facility or asset must do.

  2. Stage 2

    Test the options

    Feasibility studies, site due diligence and options analysis, so the preferred approach is chosen on evidence.

  3. Stage 3

    Build the case

    Cost estimates, lifecycle costs and a business case that leadership and funders can approve.

  4. Stage 4

    Secure the funding

    Project Approval Requests, funding applications and the approvals needed to proceed, including BCRs.

  5. Stage 5

    Ready to build

    Delivery model, procurement documents and RFPs, so construction starts with a clear scope and budget.
